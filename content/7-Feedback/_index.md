## The environment

I’d definitely recommend FCAJ to a friend. I’d tell them how friendly it felt to learn there. While my team and I were building Ownword, I felt comfortable asking questions, and I liked being around people who were eager to learn too.

## Support from my mentor and the admins

Whenever I got stuck or had a question, my mentor and the admins took the time to help. They were patient when I needed something explained again, which made it easier to ask follow-up questions. I appreciated how specific their feedback on Ownword was. It helped me see what I could work on next.

## Learning through Ownword

I enjoyed trying out AWS services with my teammates. One problem I remember was figuring out which IAM permissions were missing. Working through it together helped me see how the services fit together.

The moment I’m proudest of is when we made Ownword available to the public for the first time. We’d spent months building it and working through problems as they came up. It felt good to open the live app and see it finally work the way we’d hoped.

## What this means for my career

I want to become an AI engineer, and I see my internship at AWS as a step in that direction. A lot of AI applications run in the cloud. Building Ownword gave me a chance to see what it takes to get an app running so other people can use it.

## A suggestion

A few lab instructions seemed out of date, and sometimes what we saw didn’t match the written steps. We worked through those differences as a team. I’d find the labs easier to follow if those steps were checked and updated more often.
