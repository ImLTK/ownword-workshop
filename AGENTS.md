## Submission and OJT completion

- **Individual submission.** Each student submits their own bilingual report and its hosted Workshop URL through FCAJ Portal → Workshop Submission. Use the published Hugo report URL. The Ownword app URL belongs inside the report as demo evidence. Follow Pending review, Approved, or Reject and act on reviewer feedback. [Portal workflow](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/instructions/workshop)
- **Group submission.** The team lead or representative also submits one technical summary Markdown file (maximum 2 MB, no images) and one complete AWS architecture XML exported from draw.io (maximum 5 MB, current official AWS icons). Use the headings Problem Statement, Proposed Solution, End-to-End Workflow, Additional (Optional), and Cost. Provide the team name and each member’s full name, student ID, and email. Teams have 1–5 members and up to three attempts. Check the cohort cutoff. [Group workflow](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/instructions/fcaj-submission)
- **Presentation and demo.** The rubric requires a Proposal / Workshop presentation and valid evidence of a running product. Accepted demo evidence includes a live demo, a clear video, or a working production link. The public handbook does not specify a face-to-face session, a mentor as the audience, or whether one presentation covers the group. Check the cohort announcement or confirm the format with FCAJ. Include the cost estimate and optimization in the report and the presentation. [Workshop rubric](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/scoring/workshop) · [Standards](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/project/standard)
- **Stamp and completion.** The handbook calls for at least three months, a qualifying workshop and full report, ten valid full-time office days, three events with clear face-visible check-in photos, and three quality team posts published in AWS Study Group. It says to submit the workshop 15 days before the stamp is needed. If the stamp is needed on 14 December 2026, that puts the submission date at 29 November 2026. Confirm the actual school deadline. Apply through the Portal with the school documents. When Approved, print them and bring them to the office. At Stamped, collect them. [Project and deadline](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/project) · [Stamp eligibility](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/project/requirement) · [Stamp process](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/instructions/stamp)
- **Attendance and conduct.** Verify days in Portal Attendance History. Office check-in and checkout are both required. Book before 17:00 the prior day. The scoring page accepts at least 20 valid bookings and at least six slots for full Office points. Confirm with FCAJ before relying on that route for the stamp because the attendance regulations specify ten full-time days. Bitexco OJT events require both Portal and Luma/Meetup registration. Follow the office rules and complete the acknowledgement as directed. [Attendance rules](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/regulations/diligence) · [Check-in and checkout](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/instructions/attendance) · [Event registration](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/events/instructions/register-ojt) · [Acknowledgement](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/review/acknowledgement)

## Report and grading requirements

- Provide English and Vietnamese versions of all main content using the FCAJ template. Include student details, Daily Worklog across Weeks 1–12 with results and references, Proposal, three original published team blogs, three personal event reports with usable photos, Workshop, Self-Evaluation with specific ratings and comments, and Sharing and Feedback. Proposal, Workshop and blogs can be shared in the group. Worklog, events, self-evaluation and feedback are personal. [Report contents](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/project/content)
- The workshop should cover a real AWS use case with at least three AWS services, prerequisites, a clear original AWS diagram matching production, data flow and service choices, reproducible deployment commands and screenshots, verification, cost estimate and optimizations, cleanup, and demo evidence. Keep credentials, tokens, account identifiers and private learner data out of public evidence. [Technical requirements](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/project/requirement) · [Workshop standards](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/project/standard)
- Architecture detail awards 0.5 each for the diagram and explanation, operational excellence, security, reliability, performance efficiency, and cost optimization. Sustainability carries zero points. Workshop / Presentation is holistic at 1.5. Missing the architecture or the presentation, or copying an existing submission, zeros the Proposal / Workshop category. Missing COST zeros the presentation. Missing product evidence zeros Demo. Production mismatch caps Architecture at half the earned points. A fully AI-generated architecture loses 1.0 point and can incur other rubric deductions. [Rubric](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/scoring/workshop) · [Deductions](https://hcm-rules.awsfcaj.com/scoring/deductions)
- Missing Blog or Daily Worklog zeros that category. The overview lists 1.5 for Worklog / Blog, 4.5 for Proposal / Workshop, 2.0 for Attitude / Compliance, 1.5 for Attendance and 0.5 Bonus. Bonus requires full Attitude and Attendance plus architecture, demo and presentation. Published Worklog and Architecture component totals conflict with their detailed tables. Confirm any score calculation with FCAJ.
